# Group Work 2: Customer Loyalty Program

## Objective

Analyze the accounting treatment of a customer loyalty program under IFRS 15.

The objective was to determine how loyalty points affect revenue recognition, identify separate performance obligations, allocate transaction prices, and account for contract liabilities arising from future redemption rights.

## Case Background

Supermarket AG operated a customer loyalty program that awarded points based on customer purchases. Customers could redeem these points for discounts on future purchases.

The analysis focused on whether the loyalty points represented a separate performance obligation and how revenue should be recognized when points were earned, redeemed, expired, or when the program was discontinued.

## Analysis Performed

* Identification of performance obligations
* Assessment of material rights under IFRS 15
* Transaction price allocation
* Contract liability accounting
* Revenue recognition analysis
* Journal entry preparation
* Financial statement impact analysis

## Key Findings

### Performance Obligations

The customer loyalty points were treated as a separate performance obligation because they provided customers with a material right that would not have existed without the original purchase.

### Revenue Allocation

A portion of the transaction price was allocated to the loyalty points and initially recognized as a contract liability rather than immediate revenue.

### Revenue Recognition

Revenue associated with loyalty points was recognized when customers redeemed points, when points expired, or when the program ended and no further obligations remained.

## Key Learning

This project strengthened my understanding of multi element contracts, deferred revenue, material rights, transaction price allocation, and the impact of customer incentive programs on financial reporting.

## Real World Relevance

Many companies operate customer loyalty programs, reward schemes, and membership programs. Accountants must determine how much revenue can be recognized immediately and how much must be deferred until future obligations are satisfied. Incorrect treatment can materially affect reported revenue and profitability.
