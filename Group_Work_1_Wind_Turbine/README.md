# Group Work 1: Wind Turbine Construction Contract

## Objective

Analyze the revenue recognition treatment of a long term wind turbine construction contract under IFRS 15.

The objective was to determine whether revenue should be recognized at a point in time or over time and assess the impact on revenue, profit recognition, journal entries, and financial statements.

## Case Background

WindCorp AG entered into a multi year contract to construct and install a wind turbine for a customer.

Two scenarios were evaluated:

* Scenario 1: Construction primarily performed on WindCorp's premises.
* Scenario 2: Construction performed on the customer's premises.

The analysis focused on IFRS 15.35 and the criteria for recognizing revenue over time or at a point in time.

## Analysis Performed

* Assessment of performance obligations
* Evaluation of transfer of control
* Point in time versus over time revenue recognition
* Revenue calculation using percentage of completion
* Journal entry preparation
* Financial statement impact analysis

## Key Findings

### Scenario 1

Revenue was recognized at a point in time because the customer did not obtain control of the asset during construction.

### Scenario 2

Revenue was recognized over time because the asset was constructed on the customer's premises and the customer controlled the asset as it was created.

### Financial Impact

The timing of revenue recognition significantly affected reported revenue, profit, contract assets, and contract liabilities across the construction period.

## Real World Relevance

Construction, engineering, and infrastructure companies frequently face revenue recognition challenges. Understanding whether revenue should be recognized over time or at a point in time is critical because it directly affects profitability, financial reporting, and investor assessment of company performance.
