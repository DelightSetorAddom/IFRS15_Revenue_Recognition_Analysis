# IFRS 15 Revenue Recognition Analysis

## Overview

This repository contains IFRS 15 revenue recognition case studies completed as part of a group assignment in the Corporate Reporting course at the University of Würzburg.

The project focuses on applying IFRS 15 principles to real world business scenarios involving long term construction contracts and customer loyalty programs. The analysis includes revenue recognition assessment, identification of performance obligations, contract asset and liability accounting, journal entries, and financial statement impacts.

## Project Structure

### Group Work 1: Wind Turbine Construction Contract

Analysis of revenue recognition under IFRS 15 for a long term wind turbine construction contract. The case evaluates whether revenue should be recognized at a point in time or over time and examines the resulting financial statement impacts.

### Group Work 2: Customer Loyalty Program

Analysis of customer loyalty points as a separate performance obligation under IFRS 15. The case evaluates transaction price allocation, deferred revenue recognition, contract liabilities, and loyalty point redemption.

## Key Learning Outcomes

Through these case studies, I strengthened my ability to:

* Apply the IFRS 15 five step model
* Assess performance obligations
* Distinguish between point in time and over time revenue recognition
* Allocate transaction prices across multiple obligations
* Account for contract assets and contract liabilities
* Prepare accounting journal entries under IFRS
* Analyze the financial statement impact of revenue recognition decisions
